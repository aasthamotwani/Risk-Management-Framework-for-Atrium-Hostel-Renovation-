# Risk-Management-Framework-for-Atrium-Hostel-Renovation-
Project Overview
**Role:** Project Manager (Master's Coursework) 
**Budget:** $5 Million (Fixed)  
**Timeline:** April 2022 – December 2023 (20 Months)  
**Objective:** Execute a complete renovation of the Atrium Hotel within a live operational environment, ensuring a hard launch deadline of December 2023.

This repository contains the comprehensive Project Management artifacts used to govern the renovation. The framework emphasizes **Quantitative Risk Analysis** and **Operational Continuity**, designed to keep the hotel open and revenue-generating during construction.

## Key Achievements & Metrics
* **Operational Continuity:** Designed a phased construction strategy (floor-by-floor) that maintained **~80% operational capacity**, preventing full facility shutdown.
* **Risk Reduction:** Utilized Pre- vs. Post-Mitigation Heat Maps to model a **70% reduction in Critical Path risk exposure** (Score 10 → 3).
* **Schedule Recovery:** Safeguarded **3 weeks of schedule buffer** by proactively mitigating top-tier risks, protecting **~12% of the contingency budget** from delay-related overruns.

## Repository Contents

### 1. Risk Management Plan (Final Version)
* **Description:** The strategic framework defining how risks are identified, analyzed, and monitored.
* **Key Feature:** Includes the governance structure and escalation matrix used to facilitate rapid executive decision-making.

### 2. Quantitative Risk Model ("Risk Calculation")
* **Description:** The analytical engine behind the project's risk strategy.
* **Key Feature:** A calculated Risk Register featuring **Pre- and Post-Mitigation Heat Maps**. It demonstrates the mathematical reduction of Risk Priority Numbers (RPN) across Safety, Schedule, and Cost dimensions.

### 3. Work Breakdown Structure (WBS)
* **Description:** A hierarchical decomposition of the total scope of work.
* **Key Feature:** Breaks down the $5M scope into manageable work packages, aligned with the floor-by-floor phased delivery schedule.

### 4. Project Scope Statement
* **Description:** The foundational document outlining deliverables, constraints, and exclusions.
* **Key Feature:** Clearly defines the "Live Environment" constraints required to ensure zero downtime for guest services.

### 5. Team Charter
* **Description:** The internal agreement defining roles, responsibilities, and communication protocols.
* **Key Feature:** Establishes the cross-functional alignment required between the construction team and hotel operations staff.

## Skills & Methodologies Applied
* **Methodologies:** PMBOK, Critical Path Method (CPM), Phased Scheduling.
* **Risk Management:** Qualitative Risk Analysis, Heat Mapping, Mitigation Planning.
* **Tools:** Microsoft Excel (Advanced Modeling), Project Management Software.
